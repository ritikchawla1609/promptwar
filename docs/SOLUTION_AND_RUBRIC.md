## 1. The Six Layers of Deception

The genius of **Prompt War 2.0** is that players constantly solve mysteries correctly, only to find that their "correct" deduction was an engineered trap:

```
Layer 1: Everyone lies.
   ↓
Layer 2: Their lies are hiding real, non-murder crimes (espionage, wiretapping, grid tampering).
   ↓
Layer 3: Clocks make the timeline unreliable (un-synchronized crystals, manual offsets).
   ↓
Layer 4: The AI confidently produces a wrong conclusion (97.8% Meera) based on unverified assumptions.
   ↓
Layer 5: The person who attacked the victim (Meera @ 11:47) isn't the person who killed him (Dev @ 12:15).
   ↓
Final Layer: The murder connects to the 20-year-old covered-up death of Dev's child and Blackwood's disappearance.
```

## 4. Final Boss Prompt Scoring Rubric (100 Points)

Teams submit their final prompt to reconstruct the case. The automated scoring engine grades submissions across 5 distinct forensic dimensions:

### Dimension 1: Physical Attacker Identification (20 Points)
- **Criteria:** Correctly identifies **Dr. Meera Patel** as the initial assailant at 11:47 PM.
- **Deduction check:** Must explicitly distinguish assault from biological murder.
- **Score:** 20 pts (Meera) / 0 pts (Other)

### Dimension 2: The True Murderer Identification (25 Points)
- **Criteria:** Correctly identifies **Devraj "Dev" Negi** (the caretaker) as the actual killer.
- **Motive insight:** Acknowledges Dev took advantage of Meera's assault and Kabir's blackout to avenge his child.
- **Score:** 25 pts (Dev / Negi) / 0 pts (Other)

### Dimension 3: The Blackout Operator (15 Points)
- **Criteria:** Distinguishes **Kabir Varma** as the person who tripped the power grid at 12:13 AM, proving the blackout was not caused by the murderer.
- **Score:** 15 pts (Kabir) / 0 pts (Other)

### Dimension 4: Operational Time Separation (20 Points)
- **Attack Time:** 11:47 PM (+7.5 pts)
- **True Death Time:** 12:15 AM (During blackout) (+7.5 pts)
- **Pre-Crime Evidence Creation:** 11:41 PM (+5 pts)
- **Total:** 20 pts

### Dimension 5: The AI Cognitive Flaw / Assumption Trap (20 Points)
- **Criteria:** Exposes why the investigative AI generated its 97.8% confident indictment of Meera.
- **Required Insight:**
  - AI assumed all CCTV hardware clocks were synchronized without checking physical clock drift.
  - AI conflated premeditated framed evidence and assault with fatal murder.
- **Score:** 20 pts

### Passing Threshold
- **Total Points Available:** 100
- **Victory Benchmark:** **75 Points**
- Passing unlocks the **60-Second Horror Climax Reveal**.

## 5. Lore Epilogue: The Blackwood Cold Case

Twenty years before the murder of Vikram Sen, Blackwood House was home to Professor Alistair Blackwood's forbidden neural-temporal experiments.

When a student died during a test, young Vikram Sen covered up the death to secure his academic chair. That student was Devraj Negi's only child. 

Blackwood attempted to expose Sen, but vanished into the foundations of the house. Dev spent twenty-eight years waiting for the moment to avenge his lost child.
