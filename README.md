## 🍪 Round 1 Overview: Dalgona Prompt

Participants face a high-stakes challenge inspired by the Dalgona test:
1. **The Walkthrough**: Interactive onboarding teaching the 3 pillars of scoring.
2. **Mission Brief**: Scenario briefing with clear business objectives and constraints.
3. **The Cut**: Surgical 2D canvas needle cutter to carve genuine context signals out of a honeycomb wafer while avoiding 4 deceptive traps and 3 trivia noise clues.
4. **Prompt Forge**: 5-minute timed buffer where participants synthesize surviving clues into an authoritative prompt.
5. **AI Firmware Simulator**: Real-time simulation testing prompt feasibility and revealing whether toxic traps were detonated.
6. **Result & Score Audit**: 100-point transparent mathematical ledger:
   * **Context Cut Precision** (0–40 PTS)
   * **Prompt Engineering Quality** (0–35 PTS)
   * **AI Strategy Feasibility** (0–25 PTS)
   * Official locked verdict for event judges.
